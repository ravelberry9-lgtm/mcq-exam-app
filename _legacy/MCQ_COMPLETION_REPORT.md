### Question Design Philosophy
Each question follows the principle of:
1. **Conceptual Understanding**: Tests knowledge beyond memorization
2. **Real-world Application**: Connects to current events and policies
3. **Multi-layered Analysis**: Requires synthesis of multiple concepts
