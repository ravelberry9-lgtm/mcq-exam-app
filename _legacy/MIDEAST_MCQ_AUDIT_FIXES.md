C) Three (Arafi, Pezeshkian, Mohseni-Ejei)  ✓ CORRECT

President Masoud Pezeshkian, and Supreme Court Chief Justice Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei. Ali Larijani, 
Secretary of Supreme National Security Council, served as de facto leader during this period until his
