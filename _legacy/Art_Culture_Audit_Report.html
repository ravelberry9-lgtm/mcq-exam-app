  • Perini Shivatandavam — ancient Kakatiya-era dance revived in Telangana (but tested for historical AP)

Partially Covered

The notes cover Pallava, Chola, Hoysala and Vijayanagara dynasties. But AP-specific temples that appear in AP High Court exams are not detailed enough:

  • Tirumala Venkateswara Temple — Most visited religious site in the world; Dravidian style; Tirupati district
  • Srisailam (Mallikarjuna) Temple — One of 12 Jyotirlingas + Shakti Peetha; Nandyal district
  • Simhachalam Temple — Narasimha Swamy; blend of Orissan + Dravidian styles; Vizag
  • Lepakshi Temple (Veerabhadra Temple) — Vijayanagara period; hanging pillar; Anantapur district
  • Undavalli Caves — Rock-cut caves; Vishnu reclining statue; Amaravati area
  • Amaravati Stupa — Major Buddhist site on Krishna river; AP's own Amaravati School of sculpture origin
  • Borra Caves — Natural limestone caves; Vizag district

P2 HIGH   Kakatiya Art & Architecture — Missing

Missing

Kakatiya dynasty is crucial for AP/Telangana history and art exams:

  • Ramappa Temple (Rudreshwara Temple) — UNESCO WHS 2021; built by Kakatiya general Recharla Rudra; famous "floating bricks" (lightweight porous bricks); Warangal, Telangana
