### 3. `Study_Notes/ch02a_paintings_prehistoric_mural_miniature.html` + `MCQs/ch02_mcqs_paintings.html` — Shah Abbas vs Shah Tahmasp I
- **Error:** Both files stated Humayun hired painters "from the court of **Shah Abbas**" in Persia
- **Why wrong:** Shah Abbas I reigned 1588–1629. Humayun died in 1556. Humayun could not have been at Shah Abbas's court. The correct Persian ruler who sheltered Humayun (1543–1545) and at whose court he met painters was **Shah Tahmasp I** (reigned 1524–1576)
- **Wikipedia source:** Mir Sayyid Ali article explicitly states "While Humayun was staying in Tabriz, he became acquainted with the artists... While Humayun was staying in **[Shah Tahmasp's]** court, two of them were invited... Abd al-Samad and Mir Musavvir [and Mir Sayyid Ali]"
- **Fix (study notes):** "Shah Abbas' court" → "Shah Tahmasp I"; added exam note warning about the Shah Abbas trap; added Wikipedia-consistent detail that Humayun first invited Mir Musavvir (father), but ultimately Mir Sayyid Ali (son) joined him. ✅ Fixed
- **Fix (MCQ Q57):** Question updated from "court of Shah Abbas" → "Persian court of **Shah Tahmasp I**" ✅ Fixed
