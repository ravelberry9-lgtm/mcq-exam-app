## CORRECTIONS MADE

### ✅ **Q30099 - FIXED (Larijani Contradiction)**

**ORIGINAL (INCORRECT):**
```
"Ali Larijani, Secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, 
announced a temporary leadership council after Khamenei's death."
```

**CORRECTED:**
```
Question: "Following Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei's death on February 28, 2026, 
Iran formed a temporary leadership council to oversee the transition. 
This council consisted of how many members?"

Options:
A) One (Khamenei's son)
B) Two (President + Parliament Speaker)
C) Three (Arafi, Pezeshkian, Mohseni-Ejei) ✓ CORRECT
D) Seven (full cabinet)

Explanation: A three-member temporary leadership council was established with:
1. Ayatollah Alireza Arafi
2. President Masoud Pezeshkian
3. Supreme Court Chief Justice Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei

Ali Larijani served as de facto leader from Feb 28-March 17, 2026, 
until his assassination by Israel on March 17, 2026.
```

**What Changed:** Now tests understanding of Iran's succession structure rather than testing whether a person could announce something after being killed.

### ✅ **Q30051 - REFRAMED (Less Trivial)**

**ORIGINAL (WEAK):**
```
"Which is NOT an IRGC component?"
A) IRGC Ground Forces
B) IRGC Aerospace Force
C) IRGC Navy
D) IRGC Naval Aviation Wing ✓ (tests specific naming)
```

**REFRAMED:**
```
Question: "The IRGC (Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps) operates 
several main branches/components. Which of the following is NOT one of them?"

Options:
A) IRGC Ground Forces
B) IRGC Aerospace Force (missiles and drones)
C) IRGC Navy
D) IRGC Air Force (as a separate branch from Aerospace) ✓ CORRECT

Explanation: The IRGC has four main branches: 
1. Ground Forces (land operations)
2. Navy (maritime operations)
3. Aerospace Force (unified air operations, missiles, drones)
4. Quds Force (special operations, overseas)

There is NO separate "IRGC Air Force" — aviation falls under Aerospace Force.
```

**What Changed:** Now tests understanding of organizational structure, not memorization of specific naming conventions.
