**CORRECTED TO:** "Following Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei's death on February 28, 2026, Iran formed a temporary leadership council. This council consisted of how many members?"

- B) Two (President + Parliament Speaker)
- C) Three (Arafi, Pezeshkian, Mohseni-Ejei) ✓ **CORRECT**
- D) Seven (full cabinet)

**Explanation:** A three-member temporary leadership council was established:
1. Ayatollah Alireza Arafi
2. President Masoud Pezeshkian  
3. Supreme Court Chief Justice Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Ejei

Ali Larijani served as de facto leader from Feb 28-March 17, 2026, until his assassination by Israel.

**Why better:** Tests understanding of Iran's succession structure, not logical contradictions.
