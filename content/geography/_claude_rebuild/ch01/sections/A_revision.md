## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **The term is given but its cause or family is missing.** A term is named without its mechanism or its siblings: Solar eclipse, Revolution, Prolate, the other "fathers", the sub-branches, the 1° test, Potential.
2. **Translated sentence shapes.** English colons and semicolons ("అంచనా చెప్పాడు:"), modifiers stacked before the noun (the Geoid definition, the Gauss sentence), calques (లోబడి), and Sanskrit-heavy words where she knows the English or a plain Telugu word (సమతలం, పరిపూర్ణమైన, దీర్ఘగోళం).
3. **Numbers that do not add up, and exam blocks written as answer keys.** Bracketed figures did not match their claim (160 m and 1%, 20%, 42 vs 43, the "average" 6,371), and some lines were option-elimination residue instead of a rule she can apply.
