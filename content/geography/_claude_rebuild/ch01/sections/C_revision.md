## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Translated sentence shape**: subject at the end after a modifier chain, and English idioms in Telugu words ("comes at a price" → ధర, "strength/weakness" → బలం/బలహీనత, "-ది" endings). Fact boxes were the worst — they compressed the paragraph into one stacked noun phrase.
2. **Names without the story**: people (Gall, Peters, Anaximander, Hecataeus, Roger II, Sikdar, the Siddhanta astronomers) and institutions (East India Company, Surveyor General) dropped in with no who/why, so the reader could not answer "why is this person here?".
3. **Exam blocks that teach guessing instead of the answer**, plus missing family/standard exam names (Rhumb line, NDVI, NATMO, Zabt, Imago Mundi, Tabula Rogeriana, Mackenzie) — the paragraph was correct but stopped one fact short of what the examiner asks.
