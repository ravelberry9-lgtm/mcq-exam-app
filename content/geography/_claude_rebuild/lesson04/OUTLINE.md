## Fact ledger (source key: OL = old lesson; ICS = ICS Chart v2024/12; USGS; IRIS; NCERT XI Fundamentals of Physical Geography ch.3–5; GSI; CGWB AP; V = writer must verify before use)

### `#interior-evidence`
- Direct: mines, boreholes, volcanic material (p25-f2, f8). OL, NCERT.
- Indirect: seismic waves, gravity, magnetic, heat flow, (electrical) (p25-f3, p26-f5, f6). NCERT.
- Kola Superdeep Borehole ~12.262 km, Russia, 1989 (p25-f5). OL; widely cited — V year.
- Drilling limits: temperature, pressure, engineering (p25-f6).
- Trieste, Challenger Deep, 1960, Don Walsh & Jacques Piccard (p25-f4). Standard.
- Meteorites (chondrites) as indirect evidence of composition (p42-f14) → detail in `#chondrite`.
- Why interior matters for geography (p25-f1) — one sentence.

### `#depth-changes`
- Geothermal gradient = temperature change per unit depth (p25-f9); "1°C per 32 m" is a rough shallow-crust figure only (p25-f10); gradient varies, not fixed 12/2/1 °C/km (p25-f11). NCERT gives 1°C/32 m.
- Centre ~5,000–6,000 °C (p25-f12). OL; USGS ~5,200 °C / some ~6,000 — V; write "సుమారు 5,000–6,000°C".
- Density increases with depth, jumps at boundaries (p25-f13); mean density 5.51 g/cm³ (p25-f14) — NASA fact sheet 5.513.
- Inner core density ~13 g/cm³, not 17 (p25-f15). PREM ~12.8–13.1.
- Pressure = force per unit area (p26-f1); increases due to overlying weight (p26-f4); 1 atm = 101,325 Pa (p26-f2, optional one line).

### `#seismic-evidence`
- P = compressional/longitudinal body wave, through solids, liquids, gases, fastest, arrives first (b04-013, p35-f1, f2).
- S = shear/transverse, particles ⟂ propagation, not through liquids (no shear rigidity), slower than P (b04-014, p35-f4, f5, f6).
- Speeds vary with elastic properties, density, state (p27-f3, p35-f3).
- Body waves = P and S (p35-f9). Surface waves exist, arrive last, owned by Ch5 (arrival order P→S→surface, p35-f17 — teach order only).
- S shadow zone beyond ~103° (p35-f8); P shadow zone ~103°–140° (p35-f7). IRIS / NCERT (NCERT: 105°–145° for P) — **changing/model-dependent, see block**.
- Oldham 1906 recognised core (p27-f18); Lehmann 1936 inner core (p28-f1). Standard.
- Moho named after Andrija Mohorovičić (1909) — standard; Gutenberg 1914 — V.

### `#crust`
- Crust = outermost compositional layer (b04-001); thin, rocky (p26-f11).
- Oceanic 5–10 km (p26-f12); continental 30–70 km, avg ~35 (p26-f13); thicker under mountains (roots) (p26-f14). USGS/NCERT (NCERT: oceanic ~5 km, continental ~30 km, Himalaya ~70 km).
- Crust <1% of Earth's mass (p26-f15) — V (~0.5%).
- Oceanic: basalt, gabbro, denser; continental: granitic, less dense (p27-f1, f2, p26-f18). Densities ~2.7 vs ~3.0 g/cm³ (NCERT 2.7 crust avg) .
- SIAL = Si+Al (p26-f7); SIMA = Si+Mg (p26-f8) — old Suess terms.
- Conrad discontinuity: upper/lower continental crust (p26-f17).
- Moho = crust–mantle boundary (b04-007, p27-f4).
- Crustal elements by mass (p28-f13..20, p28-M4, M5): O 46.6, Si 27.7, Al 8.1, Fe 5.0, Ca 3.6, Na 2.8, K 2.6, Mg 2.1 (Mason table, NCERT). Whole Earth most abundant: iron; crust: oxygen (p28-f12).
- Crust supports settlement, farming, mining (p26-f16) — one sentence.

### `#mantle-core`
- Mantle = silicate layer between crust and core (b04-002); Moho to ~2,890 km (p27-f5); ~68% of mass (p27-f7, V ~67%); density ~3.3–5.6 g/cm³ (p27-f8); mostly solid but flows slowly (p27-f6); ~660 km mineral phase change = upper/lower mantle (p27-f10); lower mantle solid under pressure (p27-f11); convection driven by internal heat (p27-f13).
- Gutenberg discontinuity ~2,890 km mantle/outer core (b04-008, p27-f14).
- Core radius ~3,480 km (p27-f15); iron + nickel + lighter elements (p27-f16); NIFE (p26-f9).
- Outer core liquid iron alloy (b04-003, p28-f2), 2,890–5,150 km → thickness 2,260 km (p28-f3); generates magnetic field (geodynamo) (p27-f17).
- Inner core solid due to pressure (b04-004, p28-f4); radius ~1,220 km (p28-f5) (old books 1,070/1,250 — trap). Lehmann discontinuity ~5,150 km.
- Barysphere = old term for dense centre (p28-f10).

### `#mechanical-layers`
- Compositional vs mechanical classification (p28-f6).
- Lithosphere = crust + rigid uppermost mantle (b04-005, p27-f12, p28-f7); ~100 km — V.
- Asthenosphere = weak, ductile upper mantle, mostly solid (b04-006, p27-f9, b04-030); thickness varies (p28-f8); NCERT: up to ~400 km.
- Mesosphere = stronger mantle below asthenosphere (p28-f9).
- Magma chambers/volcanism source in asthenosphere — one line (NCERT).

### `#mineral-rock`
- Mineral: natural, inorganic, solid, ordered structure, definite composition (p42-f1).
- Rock: aggregate of one or more minerals (p42-f2); rock salt–halite, quartzite–quartz (p42-f3); dunite–olivine (p42-f4); mica = silicate mineral group, not flint (p42-f22); mica vs mica schist (p43-f18).
- Three groups by origin (p42-f5).
- Mohs: talc 1 (p43-f24), diamond 10 (p43-f23). Full scale: talc, gypsum, calcite, fluorite, apatite, orthoclase, quartz, topaz, corundum, diamond (standard).

### `#igneous`
- Igneous = cooled magma/lava (b04-009, p42-f6); magma = molten rock below surface (b04-012); "primary rocks" (NCERT).
- Plutonic: slow cooling, large crystals (p42-f7); granite, syenite, gabbro, diorite (p42-f10).
- Hypabyssal: shallow intrusive (p42-f8); dolerite/diabase in dykes, sills (p42-f11).
- Volcanic: fine crystals or glass (p42-f9); basalt, rhyolite, andesite (p42-f12); basalt 45–52% SiO₂ mafic, andesite 57–63% intermediate, rhyolite silica-rich (p40-f3, f4, f5; NPS chart). Trachyte, phonolite distinct types (p42-f13).
- Vesicles from gas bubbles (p42-f15); fossils rare (p42-f16).
- Rock solidified below ground stays "intrusive" when exposed (p39-f9).
- Granite coarse-grained intrusive (b04-032).
- Kimberlite = deep-mantle igneous rock, some carry diamonds (p40-f6); kimberlite rock vs diamond mineral. Wajrakarur (Anantapur, AP) (p40-f7) → fact line in `#ap-link`; Tokapal, Bastar, Chhattisgarh (p40-f8) — keep one line here.

### `#sedimentary`
- Definition (b04-010); weathering then erosion (p42-f17); lithification (p42-f18).
- Conglomerate rounded vs breccia angular (p42-f19); sandstone (p42-f23); shale, mudstone (p42-f24); loess wind silt (p42-f20); till/tillite/moraine (p42-f21).
- Chemical: rock salt from evaporation (p42-f25); gypsum, anhydrite = calcium sulphate (p42-f26); chert fine quartz, flint a form of chert (p42-f27); banded iron formation (p42-f28).
- Organic: limestone/chalk (p42-f29); coal (p42-f30); Carboniferous coal link → `#paleozoic`.
- Layers = depositional history, may be folded (p43-f1); strength varies (p43-f2); fossils preserved (p43-f3); petroleum/gas in sedimentary basins (p43-f4). NCERT: ~75% of land surface covered by sedimentary rocks but ~5% of crust volume — V, optional.

### `#metamorphic`
- Definition: solid-state change (b04-011, p43-f5); limestone → marble without melting (b04-029, p43-f14).
- Protolith (p43-f6); contact (p43-f7), dynamic (p43-f8), regional (p43-f9); metasomatism (p43-f10).
- Schist: mica alignment/foliation (p43-f11); gneiss: light/dark bands (p43-f12).
- Pairs: sandstone→quartzite (p43-f13), shale→slate (p43-f15), granite→metagranite/gneiss (p43-f16), basalt→greenschist/amphibolite (p43-f17), dunite→serpentinite/talc (p43-f19), metarhyolite/meta-andesite (p43-f20), coal→anthracite→graphite (p43-f21, f22).
- Charnockite (orthopyroxene/hypersthene granite; named after Job Charnock; St Thomas Mount, Chennai) and khondalite (named after Khond people, Odisha; Eastern Ghats) — OL + GSI; V names' origin.

### `#rock-cycle`
- Any group can become any other; multiple paths (OL, b04-029, b04-cycle).
- Rock → soil via weathering (one line).

### `#chondrite`
- Chondrite = stony meteorite, primitive Solar System material (p42-f14).

## Traps (max 1 per section)
- `#interior-evidence`: Kola borehole depth vs Earth's radius — deepest hole only ~0.2% of radius (no direct sample of mantle).
- `#depth-changes`: "1°C per 32 m holds all the way to the centre" → it does not.
- `#seismic-evidence`: S-wave shadow zone means outer core is liquid — not "whole core liquid" (inner core solid).
- `#crust`: Oxygen is most abundant in crust, iron in whole Earth; element vs mineral (quartz is not an element).
- `#mantle-core`: Inner core hotter yet solid — because of pressure.
- `#mechanical-layers`: Asthenosphere is not a magma ocean; mostly solid.
- `#mineral-rock`: Mica (mineral) vs mica schist (rock) / quartz vs quartzite.
- `#igneous`: Granite exposed at surface is still intrusive (plutonic).
- `#sedimentary`: Conglomerate (rounded) vs breccia (angular).
- `#metamorphic`: Metamorphism does not melt the rock (melt → igneous).
- `#time-scale`: Tertiary is no longer a formal period.
- `#paleozoic`: Trilobites are arthropods, not fish.
- `#meso-cenozoic`: Holocene start ≠ start of humans.
- `#ap-link`: Dharwar is a rock system, not Dharwad district.
