- WMO Climatological Standard Normal = averages over 30 consecutive years; current 1991–2020; updated each decade; 1961–1990 retained as reference for long-term change assessment. WMO. [P055-001 O14-007]
- Temperature anomaly = observed − stated reference mean (e.g. +1.2 °C relative to 1850–1900). [O14-008]
- Normal ≠ anomaly (statement items). [O14-014 O14-019]
- Short-term cold spells occur within long-term warming — variability vs trend. [O14-023]
- IMD all-India southwest-monsoon LPA 87 cm (1971–2020 base) V.

### `#classification`
- Book: by Sun angle/temperature → 3 heat belts; with rain, humidity, winds, distance from sea, altitude → 11 regions. BK p57, p79.
- Herbertson (A. J. Herbertson, 1905) "natural regions" V.
- Köppen: first map 1884 (temperature zones), 1900 (vegetation-based), 1918 full, 1936 final; Geiger updates 1954, 1961 → Köppen–Geiger. Britannica V. NCERT-F gives groups A, B, C, D, E (H highland).
- Thresholds: A coldest month ≥18 °C; C coldest between −3 (or 0) and 18 °C; D coldest < −3 °C (or 0) and warmest >10 °C; E warmest <10 °C (ET 0–10, EF <0); B where evaporation exceeds precipitation. NCERT-F. [P095-011 P105-002: Csa/Csb, Cfb]
- Letters: f, m, w, s; W, S; T, F; third a (hot summer >22 °C), b (warm), c (cool), d (very cold winter), h (hot dry), k (cold dry). NCERT-F.
- Thornthwaite 1931 and 1948 (potential evapotranspiration); Trewartha 1966/1980. V.
- Updated high-resolution Köppen maps (Beck et al. 2018, Scientific Data; 2023 future maps) V.
