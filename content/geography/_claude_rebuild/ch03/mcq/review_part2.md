### The one systematic problem (fix this first)

In at least 17 single-answer questions the correct option is the only one that breaks the pattern of its siblings: the distractors end in "కాబట్టి" / "ఎందుకంటే ..." and the key is a bare clause with a semicolon, or the reverse. IDs: 090, 093, 102, 106, 110, 119, 127, 129, 132, 142, 147, 150, 158, 159, 162, 163, 115. A student picks the odd one out and is right without knowing the topic. The writer's generator seems to append "కాబట్టి" to distractors only. Rule: give all four options the same ending and the same sentence shape (the fixes below do this). Second pattern: absolute words only in false options ("అసలు", "ఒక్కటే", "ఏదీ లేదు", "శాశ్వతంగా", "పూర్తిగా") in 096, 110, 116, 123, 150, 151, 158, 163. Third pattern: stems that say "పాఠం ప్రకారం / పాఠం చెప్పే" (118, 157) or wrap a quoted question (090, 102, 103, 147, 161); an exam never says that.
