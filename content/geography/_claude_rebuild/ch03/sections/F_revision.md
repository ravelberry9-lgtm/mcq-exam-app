## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Naming without explaining (undefined term / dangling).** Terms and people are dropped in with a label but no meaning or reason: Big Bang, fusion, Plate tectonics, Monistic/Dualistic, Hydrostatic equilibrium, Gunn, Lockyer.
2. **Mechanism skipped, and analogies that don't show it.** "It spun faster", "rings split off", "gas was gone", "compared the Leads" were all stated with no why. The analogies (dough, top-and-thread, box-plus-hourglass, lorry-plus-bus-stand) did not show the mechanism or mixed two scenes.
3. **Translated or imprecise wording that changes the science.** Calques ("పుట్టిన రోజు వేడి", "జవాబు ఆలోచించాడు", "దెబ్బతీసింది") sat alongside quiet factual slips: Angular momentum called a "force", all Apollo rocks given the oldest ages, Schmidt placed against his own definition, "250 years".
