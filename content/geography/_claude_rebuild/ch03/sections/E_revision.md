## Patterns the writer kept repeating

1. **Mechanism skipped.** The writer stated the result ("Core shrinks", "Iron gives no energy", "spin speeds up", "redshift means far") and left out the cause. There were 11 MISSING-WHY items and most CONFUSING ones came from this.
2. **Translated passives and stacked modifiers.** Examples: "కుక్కబడి", "బంధించబడి", "కట్టుబడి", "నడిపేంత Temperature రాదు", the stutter "అది ఆ పరిష్కారమే", and Sanskrit words such as "అసంబద్ధం" and "పరమాణు కేంద్రకాలు".
3. **Name or term dropped without the story or definition.** Examples: Galaxy, Binary, Supergiant, Axis, Thermonuclear, General relativity, Schwarzschild radius, and Uranus/1915/Itoh without the person or year. Family lists were also missing: spectral classes, giants, nebulae, quarks.
