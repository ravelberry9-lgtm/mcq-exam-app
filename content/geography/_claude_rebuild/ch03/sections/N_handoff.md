# Handoff from N (Moon)

## For O (eclipses)
- 35: Lunar eclipse: Earth shadow on Moon; order Sun–Earth–Moon; only at Full moon.
- 35: Solar eclipse: order Sun–Moon–Earth; Moon's shadow on Earth; Sun's output does not stop; not seen everywhere on Earth.
- 35: Moon's orbit tilted to Earth's orbital plane; the two crossing points are Nodes; eclipse only when New/Full moon falls near a Node — hence not every month.
- 35: Totality: seconds to minutes; depends on shadow width/speed and observer position; near-present maximum ≈7½ min (book's 7 min 32 s is a maximum, not typical); whole eclipse incl. partial phases longer than totality; eclipses per century not constant.
- 23: Earth's curved shadow in lunar eclipse = evidence for round Earth (also ships hull-first) — Earth concept.
