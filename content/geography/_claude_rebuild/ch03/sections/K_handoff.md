# Concept K handoff — facts in my sources (14, 28, 33, 22, 23) for other concepts

## Stars / constellations (33) — concept D/C
- 33: Sun nearest star; Proxima Centauri nearest after the Sun (~4.24 ly, red dwarf, Alpha Centauri system, not naked-eye); Sirius brightest night-sky star (apparent brightness vs luminosity vs distance).
- 33: Andromeda nearest large spiral galaxy; satellite galaxies are closer.
- 33: Constellation = defined sky region; 88 IAU constellations; 12 zodiac signs and 27 nakshatras are different divisions; Big Dipper/Saptarshi = asterism in Ursa Major; Polaris in Ursa Minor, near north celestial pole; Hydra largest constellation by sky area.
- 33: Largest-star claims (UY Scuti) depend on radius estimates and sample; radius, mass, luminosity differ.

## Stellar remnants (28) — concept G/E
- 28: Classical nova vs Type Ia supernova; red, brown, black, white dwarfs; stellar mass role; black hole event horizon; Chandrasekhar limit ~1.4 M☉; Hawking radiation 1974; Wheeler popularised "black hole" 1967; Chandrasekhar Nobel 1983 with Fowler.

## Venus (28) — concept H (already in #inner-venus)
- 28: Venus "Earth's twin"; brightest-appearing planet, morning/evening star, not a star.
