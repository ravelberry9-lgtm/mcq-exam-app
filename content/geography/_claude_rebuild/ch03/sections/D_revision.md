## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **English sentence order in Telugu**: the definition comes with stacked modifiers and the noun or term last ("...కలిసిన కుటుంబం", "...చెప్పేది Latitude", "...తిరిగేవి Inferior planets"). 9 of 13 TRANSLATED items.
2. **Naming a fact without its cause or its family**: fusion, precession, altitude = latitude, no Opposition, why 12; and lists cut off with "మొదలైనవి" (rashis, nakshatras, saptarshi, bright stars).
3. **Option-elimination and "they are different" lines without the answer** ("ఆప్షన్లలో X ఉన్నా...", "ఈ మూడూ వేర్వేరు ప్రశ్నలు" with no Mass / Luminosity answer). These are R6 / R7 regressions.
