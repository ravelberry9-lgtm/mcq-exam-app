## Patterns the writer kept repeating

1. **English-sentence shape in Telugu words.** The subject comes last after a long modifier or condition, the text slips into passive voice (నెట్టబడింది), and it uses calques (అందమైన విషయం, కళ్లకు ముందు, రికార్డు కాలంతో మారింది, తిరగబడింది, ఎక్కడో). There were 15 of these.
2. **Options-talk instead of facts, and confident exam defaults with no source.** Examples: "Options లో X ఉన్నా…", "చాలా పుస్తకాలు…", "ఇటీవలి పుస్తకాలు…". This is the R7 residue pattern.
3. **Numbers with no definition, unit or arithmetic shown.** N, K, bar, Flattening, Mean radius and Volume had no unit. Totals the student can recompute disagreed (43 vs 42, 1,400 vs 1,321, 10.7 vs 10 h 39 m), and the lesson mixed digit-grouping styles.
