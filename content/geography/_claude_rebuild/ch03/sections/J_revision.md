## The three facts the caller flagged (verified on NASA/JPL, not Wikipedia)

- **SL9 1992 distance (item 47):** JPL SL9 background page: closest approach "within 1.4 Jupiter radii from Jupiter's center" on 7 July 1992, i.e. about 0.4 Rj (~28,000 km) above the cloud tops. NSSDC says "0.38 Jupiter radii (71,400 km) above the cloud tops"; 0.38 Rj is ~27,000 km, so its 71,400 km parenthetical is Jupiter's radius pasted in by mistake. The old 71,400 km was wrong; so is the reviewer's 40,000 km (that came from Wikipedia). Lesson now says ~28,000 km, from JPL.
- **Eris distance (item 25):** JPL SBDB gives a = 67.9 AU, q = 38.2 AU, Q = 97.7 AU, i = 43.9°, P ≈ 561 y. JPL Horizons gives r = 95.4 AU on 4 Nov 2026. "Currently 68 AU" was wrong: 68 AU is the average. The period has also been corrected from 557 y to ~560 y.
- **Short-period vs Jupiter-family (item 28):** JPL SBDB defines Halley-type comets as 20 y < P < 200 y, which leaves the classical Jupiter-family as P < 20 y. NASA's Kuiper Belt facts page says Jupiter-family comets come from the Kuiper Belt, where Neptune nudges them inward and Jupiter then holds them in short orbits. Halley is Halley-type and is thought to come from the Oort Cloud. The fact and exam lines are now split, and the textbook answer ("Kuiper Belt") is kept with guidance.

## Lint

There were 4 long sentences, 1 calque ("దారి తీసింది"), 1 vague "కోట్ల కొద్దీ" and 1 repeated formal term. All fixed, and "అణువు" was replaced by Atom/Molecule. These hits remain on purpose:
- "absolute claim" prompts: each one is verified (first KBO, first dwarf-planet visit, largest asteroid, first predicted comet return, and so on).
- "లెక్కలేనన్ని" for the early pieces: there is no measured number.
- తోకచుక్క / ధూమకేతువు: the alternate name is given once for recognition.

## Patterns the writer kept repeating

1. **Fact without its number, person or qualifier.** Passive "కనుగొన్నారు" with nobody named (Pluto). Belt with no AU, comets with no cut-off. "ప్రస్తుతం" used for an average distance. "Short-period = Jupiter-family" flattened. A number taken from a source without a sanity check (71,400 km = Jupiter's radius).
2. **Terms used before or outside their definition, and other concepts re-taught instead of linked.** Solar System, Comet, Radius, Ion, IAU, Silicate and Mesosphere layers were used before being defined. Meanwhile the IAU rules and AU, which other sections own, were explained a second time.
3. **The "why" step skipped or faked with a connector, and analogies that fail the home test.** "అందుకే Asteroid అనే పిలిచారు" is a non sequitur. "Neptune pushes → Jupiter-family" had no Jupiter step. "Gas gets Charge" had no cause. The fan, dough and ఉత్తరేణి pictures break when she tries them at home. Translated calques with colons (నిర్ణయానికి వచ్చాడు:, అర్థమైంది:) travel with these gaps.
