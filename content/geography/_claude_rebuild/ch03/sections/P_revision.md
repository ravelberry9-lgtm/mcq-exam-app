## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. Abstract verbal-noun / stacked-modifier sentences ("పెరగడం, తగ్గడాన్నే", "ఒక వైపు, అవతలి వైపు మధ్య ఆ Pull లో ఉండే తేడా") — translated-from-English shape with the subject buried.
2. Naming a set member without the family (tide types, phases, eclipse types, ocean-energy types) and pushing it to "later/Chapter 20".
3. Numbers without derivation, qualifier or consistent source (the "half", 8,000 vs 8,300 vs 12,455, Vizag 1–1.5 vs 1–2, "eight times", "2020") plus undefined labels (MW, NOAA, Chart datum).
