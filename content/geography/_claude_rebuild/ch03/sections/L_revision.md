## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. Stating a result with "అందుకే" and no mechanism (day/night, Venus 117, disc formation, 7%, seasons) — and pushing the "why" to a later section.
2. English-shaped sentences: stacked modifiers before the subject (Tycho/Kepler, Law 3 condition) and calques (కలిసి పనిచేయడం, బయటకు వచ్చాయి, తెచ్చుకోవాలంటే, అనులోమానుపాతం).
3. Using a term or a word before / outside its definition, or one word for two things (Retrograde, tilt, Perihelion, "axis" vs Major axis, దారం = thread and Orbit), plus missing family members and exam names (Ptolemy/Copernicus/Galileo, Perigee, Inertia, Eccentricity, alternate law names).
