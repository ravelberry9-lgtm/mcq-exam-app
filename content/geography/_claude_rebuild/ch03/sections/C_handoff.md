## Concept G — Sun properties and layers (source 30)
- 30: Convection = matter moving up/down carrying heat; Radiation = energy travelling as light-like radiation.
- 30: Core, visible layer (Photosphere) and Corona temperatures differ.
- 30: Energy produced by Nuclear fusion in core, reaches outside through inner layers.
- 30: Sun is a G-type main-sequence star; Hydrogen fusion in core; will become Red giant, then leave a White dwarf; not massive enough for Neutron star.
- 30: Composition by mass: Hydrogen most, then Helium; mass % and particle-number % differ.
- 30: Energy source: H nuclei fuse to He, some mass becomes energy; not chemical combustion, no oxygen needed.
- 30: Solar diameter ≈ 1.39 million km (13.9 లక్షల km), ≈109 × Earth's; radius half. (Earth–Sun distance 149.6 million km is a different measurement.)
- 30: Solar mass ≈ 1.99 × 10³⁰ kg ≈ 333,000 × Earth's; not volume ratio.
- 30: Solar volume ≈ 1.3 million Earth volumes; volume ratio = cube of diameter ratio.
- 30: Differential rotation: equator ≈25 days, poles ≈35 days (sidereal), Sun is plasma, not rigid.
- 30: Galactic year ≈ 230 million years (23 కోట్లు); book's 250 million is an older estimate; not axial rotation. (Verified: NASA Sun facts says ~230 million years.)
- 30: Core ≈ 15 million °C, very high pressure.
- 30: Radiative zone: energy moves by radiation; photons repeatedly interact; temperature varies with depth (no single 700,000 °C value).
- 30: Convective zone: hot plasma rises, cool sinks; below Photosphere; no single 20,000 °C value.
- 30: Photosphere: most visible light; ≈5,500 °C (not 7,500 °C); sunspots darker because cooler; not solid ground.
- 30: Chromosphere above Photosphere; outward order Photosphere → Chromosphere → Transition region → Corona.
- 30: Transition region: thin, temperature rises sharply between Chromosphere and Corona; may be omitted in 6-layer model.
- 30: Corona: outer atmosphere, million-degree, much hotter than Photosphere but very thin (high temperature ≠ high density).
- 30: Simple order Core → Radiative → Convective → Photosphere → Chromosphere → Corona; first three interior, last three atmosphere.
- 30: Sun's position: Orion Spur (from NASA Sun facts, seen while verifying; not in source).
