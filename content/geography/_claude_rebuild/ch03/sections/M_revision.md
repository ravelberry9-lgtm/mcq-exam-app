## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Translated English structure.** "two advantages", "one full change", "the two together work", "exactly as much as", and singular/plural slips. The sentences were planned in English and then put into Telugu.
2. **Terms and claims used before they are defined or explained.** Hemisphere, Equator, Orbit, Monsoon, IST, Solstice/Equinox meanings and Perigee were used or warned about before being taught. Claims like "దాదాపు", "తగ్గుతోంది" and "పగలు ఎక్కువ" were left without the reason.
3. **Over-general statements that clash with other sections or with the reader's own experience.** Overhead Sun for the whole hemisphere, "June summer" against the June monsoon, "coast is cooler" against hot Vijayawada, the "egg" orbit, and the noon-shadow claim. Along with this, exam-name variants (Summer Solstice, Vernal Equinox) and whole families (Latitude lines, seasons) were missing.
