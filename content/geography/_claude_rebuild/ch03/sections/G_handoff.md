## Concept R (missions) — source 38, 22
- 38: Aditya-L1 — India's first space-based solar observatory; launched 2 Sept 2023; halo orbit around Sun–Earth L1 on 6 Jan 2024; L1 ≈ 1.5 million km from Earth towards Sun (≈1% of Earth–Sun distance; not midpoint); studies solar layers, solar wind, activity; not a lander.
- 38: Parker Solar Probe (NASA) — flies through corona; studies corona heating and solar wind acceleration; "touched the Sun" = flew through corona.
- 38: Proba-3 (ESA) launched by ISRO PSLV-C59 on 5 Dec 2024; two spacecraft in formation create an artificial eclipse; coronagraph observes corona.
- 38: Solar spacecraft need not land; they measure images, radiation, particles from a distance.
- 22: WESAT payload (women's engineering college students, Kerala) on PSLV-C58/XPoSat POEM-3, 1 Jan 2024; solar irradiance and UV index; a payload, not a satellite; old key unchanged.
(I mention Parker Solar Probe in one sentence in #sun-atmosphere as the mission aimed at the coronal heating question, and Coronagraph as an instrument; mission details stay with R.)

## Concept H (Mercury, Venus, Earth) — source 38
- 38: Mercury thin exosphere; day–night temperature contrast.
- 38: Mercury sidereal rotation ≈ 58.6 d, orbit ≈ 88 d, solar day ≈ 176 d.
- 38: Natural moons: Mercury 0, Venus 0, Earth 1, Mars 2 (Phobos, Deimos).
- 38: Mercury eccentricity ≈ 0.206, highest of 8 planets.
- 38: Venus CO₂ ≈ 96.5%; strong greenhouse; hotter than Mercury.
- 38: Venus retrograde rotation (clockwise from north), revolution anticlockwise.
- 38: Venus surface mapped by radar; Maxwell Montes highest; radar colours not natural.
- 38: Earth surface ≈ 71% water, ≈ 29% land (surface area, not mass/volume).
- 38: Earth name from Anglo-Germanic word for ground/soil; Neptune Roman sea god, Mars Roman war god, Uranus Greek sky god.
- 38: Planet atmosphere and surface decide their temperatures and appearance.
