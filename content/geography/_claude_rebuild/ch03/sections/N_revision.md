## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Number or name left vague or missing** ("కొంచెం వాలుగా", "కొత్తలో", "దగ్గరలో", "ఒక్క వ్యక్తి పేరుతో కాదు", ChaSTE with no result, no lowest point). This was the most common failure: about 13 items.
2. **Result stated without the mechanism** (Tidal locking, 50 minutes leading to the table, Libration causes, why Vikram failed, why only 14 days, why perigee distance changes). The step in the middle is skipped.
3. **English-shaped Telugu**: stacked genitives, verbless "వరుస సుమారు …" fragments, calques ("ఆధునిక రూపంలో ప్రతిపాదించారు", "ఆధారంగా వాడే"), a colon-introduced "ఒక పరికరం ఉంది:", and ambiguous "దానిపై"/"ఇవి" references. Close behind: everyday words reused with a technical meaning (బరువు used for Mass, చుట్టు for both Rotation and Revolution, బింబం).
