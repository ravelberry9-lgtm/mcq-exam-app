## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Number promised, number missing**: "చాలా దగ్గరగా ఉంది", "విశ్వం ఎంత పెద్దది?" (no size), "కోట్ల కొద్దీ" (off by 1000×), "సెకనులో చిన్న భాగం". The rule should be: every size, closeness or count claim carries its number.
2. **A step of the mechanism skipped exactly where the "why" lives**: shadow angle → 1/50, Hubble's distance method, compression → heat → fusion, cooling → transparency, supernova → acceleration. The writer names the cause but jumps over the one step that makes it make sense.
3. **Family truncated to the members the source happened to mention**: the address chain without Local Group or Laniakea, two nebula types instead of three, no Blueshift, no Big Crunch/Freeze/Rip, no Earth interior, geographers with their books but without the ism the exam asks (Determinism, Possibilism, Areal differentiation).
4. **Calqued Telugu nouns and verbs**: దాచుకుంటుంది (contains), పుట్టిళ్లు (nurseries), "ఈ కనుగొన్నందుకు", "ఈయనకు పేరు" (is known as), నిలబెట్టారు (established), and అణువు used for atom.
5. **Exam blocks about wording instead of science** ("ప్రశ్నలో 'పెద్ద' అనే మాట ఉందో చూడాలి", "billions ఎంచుకోండి"), and old-vs-new figures (galaxy types 3/4, theory count 3/5, 73/23/4) given without a "పరీక్షలో ఎలా జవాబు రాయాలి" block.
6. **Facts that contradict sibling sections** (Andromeda barred vs spiral in C; "Sun becomes a planetary nebula" vs E). Writers should grep the sibling sections before finalising.
