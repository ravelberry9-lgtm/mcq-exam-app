## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **The type or family is named but not shown in full.** One example per set: one Mars moon, one Rover, three outer-planet missions called "all of them", one or two missions per Lagrange point, five types with Parker and Probe left out. The rule "Give the whole family" was broken in five places.
2. **Terms are used before they are defined, or Telugu nouns are made up for them.** Probe, Stage, Plasma, Occulter, IAU, Greenhouse effect, Launch window, Chromosphere, plus "వెలుగు శక్తి" and "ప్రయోగ వేదిక". The writer assumed school English covered these, or invented a Telugu word where she knows the English one.
3. **The "why" or the key number goes in the trap box or is skipped.** Examples: the reason for the twin asteroid, the 1977 alignment, Pluto's three rules, why Proba-3 needs two spacecraft, and why JWST is at L2. Vague amounts appeared too ("వందల రెట్లు", "కొంచెం దూరం"). An analogy contradicted the mechanism (tug of war), and one trap contradicted the body (equal pulls).
