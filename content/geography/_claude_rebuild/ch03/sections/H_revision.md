## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Name-less facts.** A mechanism or object is described but the exam term or person is missing: Solar nebula, Frost line, Habitable zone, Centrifugal force, Plate tectonics, Ecliptic, Geoid, Pettengill/Dyce, Aristotle/Eratosthenes, Curiosity/Phoenix. This is the R3 pattern again.
2. **Translated Telugu.** English idioms or stacked modifiers: "లెక్కకు రాదు", "తిప్పి పంపుతాయి", "ఈ విధంగా చెప్పడంలోనే కలిసి ఉంది", "విసిరేయబడినట్టు నెట్టబడుతుంది", a modifier chain before "శాస్త్రవేత్త", "ఎంత పదార్థం ఎంత కూరి".
3. **Explanations that contradict or skip their own premise.** The Solar-day rule broke on Venus; rust needs Oxygen on an O₂-less Mars; CO₂ was shown causing steam; "90× denser" merged with Pressure; polar ice appeared before the tilt that explains it; analogies (butter, egg-ellipse, dough on a finger) did not match the student's home.
