## Earth concept (23)
- Earth's age ~4.54 billion years by radiometric dating; oldest rock age ≠ Earth's age.
- Conditions for life: liquid water, atmosphere, sunlight, materials; magnetic field deflects charged particles (define charged particles); distance alone not enough (Venus vs Earth).
- Day–night from rotation; Oblate spheroid; equatorial diameter ~12,756 km; evidence of roundness (lunar-eclipse shadow, ship hull).

## Moon / eclipses (23)
- Moon rotation ≈ revolution period → same face; phases from changing sunlit part seen; solar eclipse (Moon in middle), lunar eclipse (Earth in middle); Moon's tilted orbit → no eclipse every month; phases are not Earth's shadow.
