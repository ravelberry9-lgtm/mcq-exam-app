## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Answer-key residue in exam boxes** — "ఆప్షన్‌లో X ఉంటే అది తప్పు" in four of six sections instead of fact + framing.
2. **Mechanism stated as a chain without the hidden step** — monsoon, precession → Sankranti, 1° Latitude = 1° altitude, aurora at poles, 90° − 23.44°: "A → C" with B missing.
3. **Terms and members introduced alone or late** — Tropics used before defined, Torrid Zone without Temperate/Frigid, Ecliptic/Solar wind/Auroral oval/Magnetic pole never named; same picture-word (పళ్లెం) reused for two different things.
