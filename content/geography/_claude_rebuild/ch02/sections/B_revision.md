## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. Term used before it is defined (Hemisphere, Meridian, Latitude, Equator, UTC/IST, Atomic clock) — often defined only inside a later trap or section.
2. Fact without its why / actor (why Greenwich, who brought IST, why Bombay refused, why tea gardens, why China one zone, why Nepal 5:45).
3. Warning or partial count without the exam answer (USA 4/9 skipping 6, "most time zones depends on the book", "some books say 4:40") — answer-key residue instead of a decision rule.
4. English-order calques ("చూడటానికి", "నడుపుతారు", "offsets ద్వారా", subjectless "జరుగుతుంది").
5. Worked examples covering only the easy case (whole degrees, positive offsets, no cross-line offsets).
