## Patterns the writer kept repeating
1. **Asks the question, skips the answer (MISSING-WHY):** the text says "ఎందుకు?" or "అందుకే" and then jumps to the fact. This happened with km vs degrees, 180°, 66.56°, the twice-overhead sun and the 82°30′ choice.
2. **English sentence structure translated into Telugu:** long chains of modifiers before the noun ("Airy తయారు చేయించిన … అనే telescope", "భూమి కొలతలు చూసే అంతర్జాతీయ సంస్థ IERS"), calques ("పెంచుతూ వచ్చారు", "తిరగబడి"), and wrong words ("పటకారు", "ప్రమాణం").
3. **Names and terms with no introduction, and exam details dropped:** Ptolemy, Airy, IERS, GPS, Climate and the Equatorial plane appear with no introduction. Exam numbers that the paper asks for (the 1884 vote, zone limits, 360 Meridians, IST facts) were left out.
