Political leadership and administrative coordination

Ministerial responsibility is political; a Secretary’s coordination is administrative. Cabinet Committees can decide delegated business subject to Cabinet review, while an informal adviser gains no ministerial office by influence alone. Confidentiality of advice does not immunise the resulting governmental act from legal review.
