Source context: GBK 66

Sovereignty extended to space boundaries

Read airspace and outer space as distinct legal categories. The maritime statute extends sovereignty to airspace over territorial waters, while Outer Space Treaty Article II excludes national appropriation of outer space. Do not infer unlimited vertical territorial sovereignty or invent a universally fixed height boundary.

Gadgil–Mukherjee: historical correction / చారిత్రక సవరణ

The NDC adopted the revised formula in December 1991. Its 60:25:7.5:7.5 weights distributed the historical remainder among non-Special Category States after earmarked deductions and the Special Category share. The performance weight of 7.5% comprised tax effort 2.5%, fiscal management 2% and national objectives 3%; special problems separately received 7.5%. This is not a universal current funding rule.
