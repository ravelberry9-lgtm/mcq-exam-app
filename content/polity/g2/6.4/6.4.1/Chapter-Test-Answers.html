1. Answer / సమాధానం: 2

Only 2 is correct. Article 12 status and Article 226 public-duty jurisdiction are different inquiries. A private body is not automatically State merely because its work matters to the public, yet a challenge connected with an enforceable public duty may have the necessary public-law character for Article 226. That does not constitutionalise all its private contracts. Identify the body, the duty and the challenged action separately. Mandamus enforces a public duty; the appropriate remedy and its conditions remain matters for judicial assessment.

2 మాత్రమే సరైనది. ప్రకరణం 12 కింద రాష్ట్ర హోదా, ప్రకరణం 226 కింద ప్రజావిధి అధికారపరిధి వేర్వేరు పరిశీలనలు. ప్రజలకు ఉపయోగకరమైన పని చేస్తుందనే కారణంతోనే ప్రైవేటు సంస్థ రాష్ట్రం కాదు. అయినా అమలుచేయదగిన ప్రజావిధికి సంబంధించిన సవాలుకు ప్రకరణం 226 కోసం అవసరమైన ప్రజాచట్ట స్వభావం ఉండవచ్చు. దీనివల్ల అన్ని ప్రైవేటు ఒప్పందాలూ రాజ్యాంగ దావాలు కావు. సంస్థ, విధి, సవాలు చేసిన చర్యను విడిగా గుర్తించాలి. మాండమస్ ప్రజావిధిని అమలుచేయిస్తుంది; తగిన పరిహారం, షరతులను న్యాయస్థానం పరిశీలిస్తుంది.

2. Answer / సమాధానం: 4

The sequence is 3–1–2. Educational admissions and public employment are separate fields, so their enabling clauses must not be interchanged. Article 15(6)(b) concerns specified educational institutions and expressly excepts the minority institutions referred to in Article 30(1). Article 16(6) concerns appointments or posts. Article 30 protects religious and linguistic minority institutions.

3. Answer / సమాధానం: 1

Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Physical comparison material differs from responses conveying personal knowledge. Selvi’s distinction prevents scientific terminology from disguising compelled testimony. But avoiding the testimonial bar is not a licence for any collection method: lawful authority, fairness and personal-liberty safeguards remain relevant. Assess Article 20(3)’s protection against compelled self-incrimination and Article 21’s procedural requirements separately. A claim of investigative usefulness does not itself erase either protection.

5. Answer / సమాధానం: 4

Option 4 separates the right from the remedy. Property is protected by Article 300A even though it is no longer a Fundamental Right. Executive convenience or public purpose alone does not replace authority of law. Article 226 extends to legal rights beyond Part III. Article 32 is the special remedy for Fundamental Rights, so a bare Article 300A allegation does not automatically satisfy its threshold. If the facts independently establish another Part III violation, that separate claim must be assessed on its own merits.

6. Answer / సమాధానం: 2

Only 2 is correct. Articles 358 and 359 operate differently. The former concerns Article 19 during Emergency on war or external aggression, with the relevant statutory and executive-action conditions. Merely enacting a law during Emergency is insufficient where the required recital is absent. Article 359 requires a Presidential order and concerns enforcement of specified rights; Articles 20 and 21 are protected from that order. Do not equate an Emergency proclamation with automatic cancellation of all rights or confuse the two provisions’ mechanisms.
