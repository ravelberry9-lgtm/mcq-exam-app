D2 Addendum — DRDO Laboratory Directory, All India

Reference use only — do not study this directory linearly. Use it for matching questions, location questions, and laboratory–technology associations, after the core concepts in D1–D7 are already mastered. This is a lookup roster, not a teaching chapter, and does not need to be memorised in full — see the Revision Priority Map (D8–D10, Tier 3) for where it sits relative to everything else in this book.
APPSC Group 2 Mains · Paper 2, Section B · Defence Technology — companion to D2 and D1. The "About DRDO" text (D1 Addendum, Paragraph 4) says DRDO is a network of around 41 laboratories and 5 DYSL. D2 already teaches the Hyderabad/Visakhapatnam cluster in depth, because that is what an Andhra Pradesh exam is most likely to test address-matching on. This document is the rest of the picture — every DRDO laboratory nationwide, organised the way DRDO itself organises them: by Technology Cluster.
L1 — Why This Document Exists

D1 already teaches the shape of DRDO's network — cluster laboratories, HR institutions, other institutions, centres of excellence, and now DYSL (D1 Addendum). What D1 does not give is the roster: which laboratory sits under which cluster, where each one is, and what it does. A question that names a laboratory you have not met — "DGRE," "SSPL," "NMRL" — is not testing a different topic; it is testing whether you know this roster. This document builds it directly from DRDO's own Technology Clusters pages, fetched laboratory by laboratory rather than summarised, so that a cluster's own listed roster is what appears below — not a guess at one.

D1 ఇప్పటికే DRDO network ఆకృతిని నేర్పింది — cluster laboratories, HR institutions, other institutions, centres of excellence, ఇప్పుడు DYSL (D1 Addendum) కూడా. D1 ఇవ్వనిది roster — ఏ laboratory ఏ cluster కింద ఉంది, అది ఎక్కడ ఉంది, ఏం చేస్తుంది అనేది. మీరు ఇంతకుముందు చూడని laboratory పేరు — "DGRE," "SSPL," "NMRL" — అడిగే ప్రశ్న వేరే topic పరీక్షించడం లేదు; ఇది roster తెలుసా అని పరీక్షిస్తుంది. ఈ document DRDO సొంత Technology Clusters pages నుంచి, ప్రతి laboratory దాన్ని విడిగా చదివి — సారాంశం కాదు — నేరుగా నిర్మించబడింది, కాబట్టి దిగువ కనిపించేది cluster సొంత జాబితా, ఊహించినది కాదు.
A note on precision. DRDO's own cluster pages list 37 named core laboratories + 5 DYSL = 42 entries as currently published (Sept 2026) — close to, but not exactly, the "~41 laboratories" the About-DRDO text gives. Treat the count the same way D1C already teaches you to treat any DRDO laboratory count: the structure is the fact, the number moves. A handful of establishments (e.g. DARE, LASTEC) are named in a cluster's own mandate text but do not appear in that cluster's numbered roster on the site as fetched — this is flagged here rather than silently resolved. Locations explicitly stated by the source are shown as given; locations not stated by the cluster page itself are marked † and are commonly-reported, not independently re-verified lab-by-lab this session.

Why / How — the causal chain this document is built on
  • Why organise this directory by Technology Cluster rather than alphabetically or by state? Because that is how DRDO organises itself — each cluster has one Director General, one mandate, and its labs report through it. Learning the cluster is learning the org chart, not just a list of names.
  • Why does D2 stay scoped to Hyderabad/Visakhapatnam instead of being replaced by this document? Because an Andhra Pradesh exam is more likely to test that specific, compact cluster in depth than the full national roster at the same depth — D2's depth and this document's breadth serve different question types.
  • Why mark some locations with †? Because DRDO's own cluster pages give an explicit city for some labs (all of NS&M) but only a DG-office address for others — marking the difference keeps this document honest about what the primary source actually states versus what is commonly reported elsewhere.

🎯 Traps & Confusables
The trapThe correction
DYSL are a separate bucket, outside the four/five original categoriesEach DYSL sits inside a Technology Cluster (e.g. DYSL-AI is part of MCC) — it is not a freestanding fifth network, just a newer lab type distributed across existing clusters.
All of DRDO's missile work happens at one locationDesign and guidance sit at DRDL/RCI/ASL (Hyderabad), but terminal ballistics sits at TBRL (Chandigarh) and test-firing at ITR (Chandipur, Odisha) — the MSS cluster spans three states.
DMRL and DLRL are the same laboratory because the names look alikeDMRL (Defence Metallurgical Research Laboratory, materials/armour, NS&M cluster) and DLRL (Defence Electronics Research Laboratory, EW, ECS cluster) are different labs in different clusters — only the first two letters differ.
A "centre" (like CEMILAC) is the same kind of unit as a "laboratory"CEMILAC certifies airworthiness; it does not itself design systems the way ADE or GTRE do — DRDO's own listings keep "laboratory," "centre" and "establishment" as distinct labels, not interchangeable ones.
